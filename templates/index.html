Clinical Rationale

Why Early Skin Health Screening Matters

Skin cancers, including melanoma and non-melanoma types, are among the most prevalent cancers worldwide. When identified in their early stages (Stage I/II), treatment success and 5-year survival rates exceed 95%.

Early Stage Intervention

Melanoma detected before deeper subcutaneous invasion has a vastly higher cure rate through simple, localized outpatient excision.

AI-Assisted Triaging

Assists general practitioners and rural clinics in categorizing suspicious lesions and referring high-risk cases for rapid dermatological assessment.

Proactive Monitoring

Empowers individuals to monitor evolving moles, document photographic changes over time, and consult doctors with organized visual records.

System Workflow

How the Screening Process Works

Designed for ease of use, security, and transparent clinical screening in three clear steps.

1

Upload a Lesion Image

Select or drag-and-drop a close-up, well-lit photograph or dermoscopy image of the skin mole or lesion in JPG, JPEG, or PNG format.

2

Automated Image Analysis

The system validates image integrity, extracts spatial features, and processes the lesion using its configured deep learning prediction engine.

3

Review & Consult Specialist

Examine the preliminary screening results, download an organized PDF summary report, and schedule a formal examination with a qualified dermatologist.

Software Engineering Architecture

Technology Behind the Platform

Constructed using modern, open-source computer vision and deep learning frameworks specified in NMIMS Experiment 01.

Convolutional Neural Network

Utilizes deep convolutional layers to detect spatial hierarchies, pigment borders, asymmetry, and color variations across dermoscopic photographs.

HAM10000 & ISIC Datasets

Referenced against the benchmark Harvard HAM10000 (10,015 dermoscopic cases) and the International Skin Imaging Collaboration archive.

Python, TensorFlow & Keras

Robust backend computational engine utilizing TensorFlow 2.x and Keras for neural tensor operations, OpenCV and Pillow for image validation.

Flask & SQLite Architecture

Lightweight, modular Flask web server with secure password hashing (scrypt), session authorization, and relational SQLite record management.

Important Medical and Clinical Disclaimer

The Skin Cancer Detection System is an AI-assisted preliminary screening tool created for educational and academic evaluation. It does not provide definitive medical diagnoses, clinical certification, or pathological findings. Skin lesions can exhibit diverse morphological features, and deep learning models may produce false positives or false negatives. Never ignore professional medical advice or delay seeking care because of any information or screening output provided by this website. Always consult a licensed dermatologist or qualified physician for formal dermoscopic examination or biopsy.
