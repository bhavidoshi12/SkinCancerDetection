Photograph Guidelines for Optimal Screening

  • Close-up framing: Center the skin lesion in the frame. Avoid wide-angle full-body or landscape photos.
  • Optimal lighting: Ensure uniform, indirect, natural lighting. Minimize heavy flash glare or deep shadows.
  • Sharp focus: Avoid motion blur. Lesion borders should be crisp and clearly distinguishable.
  • Supported formats: JPG, JPEG, and PNG files up to 10 MB in file size.
